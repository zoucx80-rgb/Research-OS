# Research OS v1.5.12 Migration

Research OS v1.5.12 is a semantic-preservation PATCH release. `CORE_API_VERSION` remains `1.0`, and there is **无数据库迁移**.

## Active presentation fingerprints

- `professional-research-view@1.7.0`
- `research-report-composer@1.4.0`
- `professional-markdown-renderer@1.4.0`

HTML and PDF fingerprints remain unchanged. Historical v1.5.08–v1.5.11 replay adapters remain frozen. The v1.5.11 runtime adapter binds a deterministic fingerprint over the complete `research_os` Python source tree, including transitive execution dependencies, with no Python-source exclusions, and retains direct dependency pins as an additional diagnostic. The expected digest lives in a strictly parsed, non-executable `.sha256` resource to avoid a self-reference or executable bypass. This deliberately conservative policy refuses historical replay after any production-source drift rather than silently changing a historical result.

## Additive contracts

- Numerical sensitivity results now retain material assumptions, model boundary, applicability and caveats.
- Monitoring thresholds retain type, source, comparison basis and applicability; analyst-defined thresholds render as research warning lines.
- Claim strength distinguishes recovery observation from trough confirmation and technical barriers from realized economic moat.
- Valuation ranges reconcile only through typed mathematical intersection, explicit cross-check bands, model disagreement or non-comparability.
- Stable fingerprints attest the actual ID-stripped sensitivity and monitoring payloads at Runtime Result, View and Document boundaries. Missing validation or a missing fingerprint fails closed.
- Field acceptance persists first-pass Markdown/HTML/PDF artifacts and records layer-attributed PASS/WARN/FAIL findings.

Existing callers may omit the new fields, but active v1.5.12 presentation refuses result-bearing sensitivities or monitoring thresholds that fail semantic-preservation validation.

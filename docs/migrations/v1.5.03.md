# Research OS v1.5.03 Migration Guide

Research OS v1.5.03 (code SemVer `1.5.3`) is the **Professional Research Integrity** release. `CORE_API_VERSION` remains `1.0`; no storage migration is required.

## What changed

### State Provenance
High-level `fundamental_state`, `valuation_state`, and `expectation_state` now carry explicit provenance. Legacy string inputs remain compatible, but they are identified as analyst assumptions rather than being presented as Research OS-derived conclusions. Provenance-aware callers may supply structured state inputs with source, evidence IDs, and method metadata.

### Evidence-driven thesis and driver lineage
The built-in thesis engine no longer asserts generic fundamental improvement merely because a supported industry plugin is present. Directional operating signals determine whether the generated thesis is improving, mixed, weakening, or insufficient. Driver nodes now carry fact-specific evidence lineage rather than attaching the full evidence set indiscriminately.

### Professional question coverage
Industry report contributions may define structured research questions with required capabilities and evidence keys. Runtime evaluates whether each question is supported, partially covered, or missing evidence. A professional-looking question is therefore not treated as professional coverage by itself.

### Quantitative presentation semantics
`ResearchViewPresenter` is now `professional-research-view@1.1.0`. Human-readable KPI output carries formatted value, display unit, period label, period days, and annualization semantics. Machine values remain unchanged and auditable.

### Event-relative expectation freshness
Expectation quality now distinguishes calendar freshness from information freshness. If a consensus vintage predates the latest material event supplied to the run, the quality assessment records `CONSENSUS_PREDATES_MATERIAL_EVENT` and the human view explains that the forecast has not absorbed the latest material information.

### Economic exposure integrity
The Router is `router@1.2.0` and suppresses the low-PPE distributor heuristic when right-of-use assets or lease liabilities indicate a lease-heavy operating model. Distributor analytics expose factoring, derecognized receivables, receivable transfers, other working-capital financing, and total financing burden without automatically relabeling those exposures as debt.

### Valuation and research view
The professional research view can expose valuation execution, assumptions, and lineage supplied by the canonical runtime. Presentation remains a one-way projection and does not recompute valuation, thesis, decision, or completion states.

## Compatibility

- `CORE_API_VERSION` remains `1.0`.
- Existing machine consumers may continue using canonical `ResearchRunResult` and `DecisionSummary`.
- `DecisionSummaryPresenter` remains available for narrow summaries.
- Complete human-facing research should use `ResearchViewPresenter` (`professional-research-view@1.1.0`).
- `ResearchCompletionGate` remains the sole source of `COMPLETE` / `INCOMPLETE`.

## Deliberately deferred

v1.5.03 does **not** add a full Hospitality Plugin, a second generic financial Decision state machine, comprehensive lease-adjusted valuation, or a Forecast subsystem rewrite. These remain future extensions rather than hidden behavior inside the current release.
